# Script Video 184 — Videofy Minimal

## Thong tin
- Tool/Repo/Skill lien quan: /repos/videofy-minimal.md
- Platform: TikTok / YouTube Shorts

## Hook (3 giay dau)
Mot tap doan bao chi lon o chau Au vua mo nguon cong cu ho dung de tu dong bien tin tuc thanh video.

## Script voiceover (ElevenLabs-ready)
[Doan 1 - van de]
Toa soan co hang tram bai viet moi ngay, lam video tay tung bai la khong kip. Can mot cach tu dong ma van giu duoc chat luong bien tap.

[Doan 2 - gioi thieu giai phap]
Videofy Minimal la ban rut gon cua he thong noi bo ma tap doan bao chi Schibsted dang dung that. No lay noi dung tu bai bao, tu viet lai thanh kich ban ngan, ghep hinh anh phu hop, tao giong doc, va cho biem tap vien xem truoc truoc khi render that.

[Doan 3 - demo cach lam]
Diem khac biet lon nhat la co giao dien bien tap rieng. Khong phai bam mot nut la ra video mu quang, ma editor duoc xem truoc kich ban, sua lai neu can, roi moi cho render. Video xuat ra ca ban doc va ban ngang cung luc, dung engine Remotion o phia sau.

May co the them nguon tin rieng cua minh, doi ten thuong hieu, mau sac, giong doc chi bang mot file cau hinh.

[Doan 4 - ket va CTA]
Neu may can ra video ngan hang ngay tu tin tuc ma van muon kiem soat duoc noi dung truoc khi dang, day la cong cu dang thu nghiem. Luu lai video de setup sau.
